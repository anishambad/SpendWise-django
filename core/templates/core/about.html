About SpendWise

SpendWise is a simple expense tracker. You add what you spend, sort it into categories, and see where your money goes each month.

It was built as a learning project with Django, to practise models, forms, templates and queries.

  • Add, edit and delete expenses
  • Group spending by category
  • Search and filter by month
  • See totals on the dashboard
